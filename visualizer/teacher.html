Lights · Grades 5–8 unit

Visualizer · Viz 0.6.15

Lights move with the beat. Students pick a color, an example such as Storm or Big boom, or Crazy. The sentence under the looks says what the sliders do. Rain splashes. Fireworks explode. The microphone stays off unless they choose device sound.

Guided start

  1. Press Play on the library beat. Read Now. Sound can stay off.
  2. Switch one Look: Bars, Kaleidoscope, Clouds, Stars, or Code.
  3. Grade 8: open a song from Beats or Score. The word should match that pattern.

Accommodations

Same plan as the unit page. Do not grade by ear. Reduced motion slows the spin. The word still updates. Device sound is optional and easy to skip. No microphone is required.

Code is a picture that grows with the beat, not a programming lesson.
