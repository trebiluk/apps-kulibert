How it works

  1. The lesson lights Play, the word, or the color dots. The button says Got it when that step is done.
  2. Pick a Look. Keys 1–5 are the first five. Keys 6–9 and 0 are the new ones.
  3. Press Play. The picture follows your song from Music. Sound can stay off.
  4. No song yet? Make one in Music. Come back. The same beats drive the lights.
