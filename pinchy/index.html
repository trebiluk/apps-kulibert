Version 1.2.1

  • Move a robot arm.

Version 1.2.0

  • Move a robot arm.
  • The arm is drawn in 3D from a fixed camera, so the base can turn.
  • Levels 1 to 6 are unchanged. Stars stay on this screen only.

Version 1.1.0

  • Move a robot arm.
  • Level 4 sorts a pile with Repeat. Level 5 sends a red square to bin A and a blue circle to bin B. Level 6 fixes a broken program with swaps.
  • Wrist up and Wrist down are in the later levels. A crash names the wrist.
  • Stars mean done, under the block par, and under the time par. They stay on this screen only.

Version 1.0.0

  • Pinchy McPinchface is the arm game.
  • Level 1 moves one joint. Level 2 picks up a part and places it. Level 3 goes to a spot from A1 to C3.
  • If the arm hits something, the note names the part and the step stays outlined. Retry keeps your program.
