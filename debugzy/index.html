Help · Easy steps

  1. Read the whole sequence once.
  2. Point to the step you think is wrong.
  3. Say why in one short sentence.
  4. Change one thing only.
  5. Press Try again (or Run).
  6. Watch the result.
  7. Pass = it works. Fail = name why · go back to step 2.
  8. Write your alias on the ticket if you keep paper proof.
