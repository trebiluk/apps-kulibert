## 1. Tone rules (every lore line, card, sign and book)
- **Real science, slightly off world.** Every strange thing has a real idea under it (glass trees = refraction, floating isles = magnetism). The wiki's science line says the real version.
- **Mystery, not menace.** Nobody is evil. Machines went quiet; nobody remembers why. Rogue robots are broken, not bad.
- **High-tech and mysterious, nothing medieval** (Diego, Oct 3). No kings, swords, castles, magic spells.
- **Short.** Lore pages ≤60 words. Signs ≤12. Kids skim.
- **No Minecraft words** (house rule): no creeper, nether, redstone, Steve, ender, etc.
- **Every culture welcome.** No religion in lore. Seasons, not holidays (Autumn, Winter); lights are for whatever a kid celebrates.

## 2. The setting: the Verge
The Verge is a beautiful land at the edge of the map, "our world, only slightly off." Glass trees split sunlight into rainbows, rock islands float on magnetic stone, salt flats hold sleeping robots, and tides run on a clock. Old machines are everywhere. Nobody remembers who built them, so people call the builders **the Makers**.
- **Bertyville** is the safe workshop town in the middle, built around the first Maker machine anyone got working: the **Clock Gate**.
- Deep under everything is the **Coreplate** (y −64), a floor of Maker metal nothing can break. Below it is only the fall back to spawn.
- **Geology Night:** once a week the Verge "breathes" and natural ore veins quietly regrow (world-3). Townsfolk say the Coreplate hums that night.
- **The Boundary Clay** (a thin stripe at y −24): a layer from the day a meteor hit the Verge long ago. It holds tiny amounts of Iridium. *Real: Earth has the same kind of layer from the asteroid that ended the dinosaurs, and it is rich in iridium.*

## 3. Characters
- **Berty** — the workshop mascot (same Berty as TechWorks and every hub app, art and colours unchanged). He runs the Bertyville workshop, hands out the first tools, and is impossible to hurt (he's the one kids fling in Bertapult and he loves it). Voice: cheerful, curious, short sentences, a little goofy, never sarcastic. He doesn't know the Makers' secret either; he wants to find out *with* you.
- **The player** — a **Verge Engineer**: a new arrival who figures out how things work. Players use aliases only.
- **Berty's Bot** — the kid's first helper robot (teal with a blue accent). Beeps, never speaks words.
- **Townsfolk bots** — shopkeeper, Trade Post clerk, Glideway conductor, job-board bot. Plain helpful lines only.
- **Rogue robots** — Maker machines that lost their instructions (like the Hawkbots). Shut them down, repair or reprogram them for parts. Never "killed."
- **Verge creatures** (Shiver, Tangler, Purrlet, Woofle, Squeakbun, Slinkoil, Featherkit) — real animals' cousins, calmed or befriended, never hurt.
- **No named human villains. No real people as characters.**

## 6. Maker Pages: the story kids piece together
Found pages (one per landmark, plus 12 hidden in ruins) collect in the Engineering Notebook. Read in order they tell this story. Kids find them in any order.
1. "We came to the Verge to learn how everything works."
2. "The Clock Gate keeps time for the whole land. Keep it turning."
3. "Light bends in the glass trees. We used it to send messages."
4. "The robots in the salt were our helpers. We gave them simple instructions."
5. "The tide is a clock too. Everything here is a machine if you look."
6. "We tied the islands down with magnets. Don't cut the guideways."
7. "Wind and sun gave us all the power we needed. Fire was a last choice."
8. "Something hit the Verge long ago. It left a thin grey line in the rock, and rare metal in it."
9. "We started a great engine: the Fusion Core. It was too big for us alone."
10. "Our robots forgot their instructions after we left. They aren't angry. They're lost."
11–19. Short pages about each machine family (tubes, quarries, vaults, bots, frames, gliders), one real idea each.
20. **Archive Door page (Legend goal 30, the last secret):** "We weren't the first, and you won't be the last. We built the Verge so the next engineers could finish what we started. The Fusion Core is yours now. Build it together."
- **Canon answer, crew only:** the Makers were engineers like the players. They left for the sky (the Hanging Isles and beyond) and left the Verge as a place to learn. The Fusion Core is their unfinished project, so the whole-class Legend goal is literally finishing the story. Never state this outright before page 20.
