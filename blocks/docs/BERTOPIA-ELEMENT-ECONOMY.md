## 1. The new Cog ladder: ranked by real rarity in the rock

### 1.1 The ladder (locked)
Rank is set by **upper continental crust** abundance (Rudnick & Gao 2003, the standard modern estimate). The upper crust is the part miners can actually reach, and it's what Bertopia's ore layers stand for. Whole-crust values (CRC Handbook) are shown alongside for honesty. 1 ppm = 1 gram in 1 tonne of rock [F31]. A tonne is about the weight of a small car.

| Tier | Cog | Symbol · Z | Upper crust (ppm) [F2] | Whole crust, CRC (ppm) [F1] | How much rarer than the tier below (from upper crust) | Property it teaches | How you earn it | What it's for |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Copper Cog** | Cu · 29 | **28** (28 g per tonne) | 60 | — | **Conductivity.** Most wires are copper because it carries electricity and heat very well [F12]. | everyday play (all the old Bronze sources) | stock, everyday looks, ladder tiers 1–5 |
| 2 | **Silver Cog** | Ag · 47 | **0.053** (53 mg per tonne) | 0.075 | ~530× rarer than copper | **Reflectivity.** Silver is the best reflector of visible light known, which is why it makes the best mirrors [F10]. It also conducts electricity a little better than copper [F11]. | skill: ★★★ or par on a Daily (max 1 a day), Weekly Build Brief, a "3 of 5" week, Tier II badges | arena skins, trails, ladder tiers 6–7 |
| 3 | **Gold Cog** | Au · 79 | **0.0015** (1.5 mg per tonne) | 0.004 | ~35× rarer than silver | **Malleability.** Gold is the most malleable metal: 1 g can be beaten into a 1 m² sheet only about 230 atoms thick [F8]. | rare: Weekly 👑 on a Bertopia-linked board, Perfect Week, teacher tournament or Contest win, Tier III badges | wings and big looks |
| 4 | **Platinum Cog** (collector) | Pt · 78 | **0.0005** (0.5 mg per tonne) | 0.005 | ~3× rarer than gold near the surface | **Catalyst + heat.** Platinum speeds up chemical reactions without being used up (its main job is in car catalytic converters), and it melts at 1,768 °C [F9]. | very rare: Season Hall of Fame top 3, 5 Weekly 👑 in one season, **Master Maker** (moved here from Titanium) | not spent. Goes in the Wallet and unlocks the Platinum look set. |
| 5 | **Iridium Cog** (LEGENDARY) | Ir · 77 | **0.000022** (22 micrograms per tonne) | 0.001 | ~23× rarer than platinum, ~1.3 million× rarer than copper | **Corrosion resistance.** Iridium is the most corrosion-resistant material known, and one of the two densest elements. A thin iridium layer in rocks worldwide is the clue that an asteroid hit Earth, possibly the one that ended the dinosaurs [F5]. | almost never: **Season Hall of Fame #1** on any Bertopia board, or **Bertodex 100%** (needs a real Iridium Speck find, ORE-TABLE §3) | not spent. Unlocks the **Starfall** look set (meteor trail, iridium-sheen suit, asteroid wings). Shown in the Wallet with a slow rainbow glint. |

**Abundance units:** every ranked/comparison value in this table is mass ppm (CRC or Rudnick & Gao). RSC/BGS values are atoms per million atoms, so they belong only in a labeled footnote, never in an abundance column.

Expected Iridium Cogs: about 1–3 per class per season. Most kids will see one only on a classmate's Wallet, which is the "almost never seen" Diego asked for.

### 1.2 Why Iridium beats Rhodium for the legendary tier
- **Rarity is a tie.** CRC lists both at 0.001 ppm [F1]. The RSC/BGS table also lists them with the same value [F3], so neither is "rarer" in the data kids can look up.
- **Kid appeal goes to Iridium:** the dinosaur-asteroid clue, a "superhero" corrosion resistance, extreme density, space hardware, and the old platinum-iridium metre bar and kilogram [F5][F29]. It also ties into the World Plan's meteor falls.
- **Rhodium stays in the game** as a Bertodex material: a by-product of refining platinum, used for the Rhodium Mirror block (real rhodium coats mirrors and headlight reflectors, and about 80% goes into catalytic converters [F7]).

### 1.3 Why Gold sits below Platinum (honest data note for the card)
- Whole-crust estimates disagree. CRC puts platinum (0.005 ppm) slightly *above* gold (0.004) [F1].
- In the **upper crust** that miners reach, platinum is probably about 3× rarer (0.5 vs 1.5 ppb) [F2]. The platinum number is very uncertain (±0.5 ppb in Rudnick & Gao Table 3), so the card says "probably."
- Mines confirm it: in 2024 the world mined about **3,300 t of gold** but only about **170 t of platinum** [F19].
- **The Platinum card says this out loud:** "Platinum is hard to measure in rock. Scientists' whole-crust estimates put gold and platinum close. In the rocks near the surface, platinum is probably about 3 times rarer. Miners dig up about 20 times more gold than platinum each year." That teaches how scientists can measure the same thing in different ways.

### 1.4 Cogs are not made of ore
Cogs are **achievement tokens named after metals**. You can't smelt a Cog, and mining gold ore never gives a Gold Cog. Ore is building material (ORE-TABLE). The only bridge from ore to Cogs is the **Trade Post** (§5), which pays **Copper Cogs only**, inside the Town Purse cap. Gold Cogs still mean "you did something great," and mining can't grind them.

### 1.5 Rules carried over
- **Metals never convert upward.**
- **Bertyville Bank change-down only:** 1 Gold → 10 Silver, and 1 Silver → 10 Copper. Platinum and Iridium are collector Cogs, so they can't be spent or changed.
- **Teacher multiplier** can only be 0×, 0.5× or 1×.
- **Bonus:** Cog metals no longer include "bronze," so the HIGHSCORE plan's **bronze/silver/gold record medals** can't be confused with a currency.

## 5. Trade Post (mild supply and demand)
**What it is:** a stall beside Tally's in Bertyville (Game world only). Tap to Open (the Open verb). Move-item ore onto the counter, then tap **Sell**. It buys **natural raw ore only**, for **Copper Cogs only**.

**Base prices (Copper per unit, before drift):**
| Buys | Base | Buys | Base |
|---|---|---|---|
| Iron Ore | 1 | Quartz | 1 |
| Raw Copper | 1 | Black Sand (titanium) | 0.5 |
| Zinc Ore | 1 | Tin Ore | 2 |
| Bauxite | 1 | Silver Ore | 4 |
| Gold Nugget | 12 | Platinum Nugget | 30 |
- **Never bought:** dirt, stone, sand, gravel, clay, coal, salt, ice and logs (commons are worth 0 and can't be sold, World Plan anti-farm rule; the live code already has `coal: sell:false`). Iridium and Rhodium are never bought, because legendary finds are kept and displayed. Crafted goods are never bought here either; town sales handle those.
- **Caps:** every Trade Post sale counts toward the **Town Purse 40 Copper/day** (shared with town sales and orders), plus **10 units per ore per alias per ET day**. Above the cap the counter says "Full for today, come back tomorrow," and the ore stays in your Bag.
- **Natural ore only:** worldgen ore carries a `natural` flag. Ore that was placed and then mined again pays 0 (World Plan: placed blocks have no value). Ore mined by the Bot or automation pays half price (World Plan automation rule).

**Drift (±20%, updated nightly at 00:00 ET, per class):**
```
M7  = units of ore o mined (natural, Game world) by this class in the last 7 ET days
T7  = class size × weekly target per kid: iron 30 · copper 20 · quartz 15 · black sand 15 · zinc 10 · bauxite 10 · tin 4 · silver 3 · gold 1 · platinum 0.2
r   = M7 / T7
price = round_to_0.5( base × clamp(1 + 0.2 × (1 − r), 0.8, 1.2) ), minimum 0.5
```
- If the class mined exactly the target, the price is base. If nobody mined it, the price is +20%. At double the target or more, it's −20%.
- **It never touches** Daily payouts, badge pays, Patents, Build Check pays or any Silver and up. Only Trade Post prices move.
- **Display:** each price shows an arrow and a word ("▲ Up 12%: not much mined this week" / "▼ Down 20%: lots mined this week"), plus a 7-day sparkline. The sparkline has a text twin for screen readers.
- **Lesson card** (Speak): "Supply and demand: when lots of something is dug up, each one is worth less. When it's scarce, each one is worth more. Real metal prices move this way too. Why is gold worth more than iron? There's far less of it in the rock."
- **Badge:** **Market Watcher**: sell an ore on a day its price is ▲ 10% or more (single, pays 10 Copper).

## 6. Patents (credit when others remix your Blueprint)
**What it is:** when someone else builds on your Blueprint, you get a **Patent**: a credit line, a little Copper and progress on the Patent Holder badge. It teaches that inventors get credit when others build on their ideas.

**Rules**
1. **Credit line:** every remix and every placed copy shows "**Original by [alias]**" on its Blueprint card and plaque. That can't be removed. A remix of a remix shows both: "Original by A · remixed by B."
2. **One Patent per unique remixer per Blueprint.** A remix counts when the remixer **saves a remix with 10 or more block changes, or places the Blueprint in the Game world.** Opening or looking pays nothing.
3. **Doesn't count:** your own remixes; **crew members** (anyone in your crew now or in the last 7 days, so leaving and rejoining a crew doesn't work); blocked players; signed-out or practice play.
4. **Pay:** 2 Copper per Patent. **Daily cap: 10 Copper (5 Patents) per ET day.** Extra Patents still count for the badge and the credit, but pay 0 that day.
5. **Pair limit:** one remixer can give you at most **3 Patents per 7 days** across all your Blueprints, so two friends can't trade Patents back and forth.
6. **Teacher import (BERTOPIA-WORLDS):** when a teacher imports your Blueprint into the Workshop, that counts as one Patent for that Blueprint. The 10-change rule doesn't apply. It pays 2 Copper into your Game-world wallet and stamps the Blueprint **"Picked for the Workshop."** If the teacher publishes it to the official map, the published area's plaque reads "Original by [alias]." The teacher earns nothing.
7. **Server-checked:** Patents need verified saves (Progression Plan §6.2). Offline remixes queue and count once when they sync, with a claim id so they never count twice.
8. **Badge:** **Patent Holder** (badge #4, replacing Remix Star): I = 1 unique remixer (10 Copper), II = 5 (1 Silver), III = 20 (1 Gold + ★ on your Blueprints). **Patent Plaque** decor block: a Blueprint with 3 or more Patents earns a placeable plaque for that build (earned only).
9. **Kid-safe:** remixers are shown by alias only. There are no comments or ratings, and no free text anywhere.
