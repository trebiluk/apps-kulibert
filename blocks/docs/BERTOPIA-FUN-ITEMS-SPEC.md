# Bertopia Fun Items + Holiday Packs (GameMaster, Oct 4 2026 6:44 AM ET)
> **Roles and worlds (6:56 AM):** "Teacher" means anyone Diego gives the TechWorks Teacher flag (staff, club officers, kid helpers), not a fixed staff list. "Creative" = the Build world (Teacher flag only) and "Survival" = the Game world (everyone). See BERTOPIA-WORLDS.md.

Rules for all: crafted or earned only, never bought with Cogs; never lost (parks/returns); alias-only; respects the player's Sound and Reduced-motion settings; works one-thumb on phone + Chromebook.

## 1. Pet Rock
- Recipe: 1 Stone + 1 Glass (googly eyes) + 1 Paint dab optional. Max 3 rocks per player per world.
- Follows at 0.5 blocks/s (very slow on purpose), wobbling and rolling, googly eyes jiggle. Tap it = happy wiggle + tiny "clack."
- Commands (tap-hold menu): Follow / Sit / Nap. If you get >32 blocks ahead it naps where it is (zZz on minimap); tap "Whistle" in the menu to wake it and it starts rolling to you. No teleport — slowness is the joke.
- Name: pick from a preset fun-name list (Rocky, Pebbles, Sir Gravel, Boulderina...), no free typing.
- Can wear tiny paint colors and mini hats; can't be harmed, pushed into lava, or picked up by others; avoids water and lava. Owner tap-holds to pocket it.
- Lesson card: "A rock has no motor. That's why it's so slow."

## 2. Gravity Hat (head slot)
- Recipe: 2 Cloth + 1 Battery Cell + 1 Glass.
- Tap the hat button: 10 s float. Fall speed capped at 1 block/s; hold Jump to rise gently (max +3 blocks over the 10 s); normal sideways speed. Soft glow ring under feet.
- Cooldown 30 s (ring timer on the button). Ending early: tap again.
- Auto-disabled inside Parkour Rush, Molten Chasm, contests, and arenas unless the course/teacher turns it on.
- Lesson: "Gravity pulls everything down. The hat pushes back a little, for a little while."

## 3. Disco Floor
- Recipe: 2 Glass + 1 Copper Wire + 1 Glow Mix → 4 tiles. No power needed.
- Step on it: the tile lights and fades over 1 s; neighbors ripple.
- Tap a tile to set pattern for the connected floor: Rainbow (default), One Color (palette), Chase, Follow the Music (pulses to a nearby Jukebox).
- Photosensitivity safe: smooth fades only, never strobe; max 3 color changes per second per tile; Reduced-motion = steady soft glow.
- Badge: Dance Floor (100 steps lit).

## 4. Bounce Block (a spring pad, not pudding)
- Recipe: 1 Steel (spring) + 2 Planks + 1 Cloth (pad) → 2.
- Walk on: bounce 4 blocks high. Jump on: 6 blocks. Falling onto it from any height: always a safe landing and you bounce at most 6.
- Stacking: stacked Bounce Blocks act as one (no extra height). Chaining pad-to-pad never gains height beyond 6.
- Crouch/hold the down button to land without bouncing.
- Parkour Rush and Chasm: only where the course builder placed them.
- Lesson: "The spring stores energy when you land and gives it back" (ties to Bertapult's stored energy).

## 5. Bobbleheads (opt-in PvP areas only)
- PvP language everywhere: "tag," "knockout," "KO." Never "kill."
- Berty Bobblehead: earned on your first arena match win; color variants for 10 / 25 / 50 wins.
- Friend Bobblehead: earned after winning 5 separate arena matches against a player on your friends/crew list (max 1 counted per match, so no farming). One bobblehead per friend.
- Only exists if PvP arenas are opened (current default: arenas closed, nobody opens PvP until Diego decides).
- Looks: the friend's current avatar and alias (auto-updates if they change either). Cannot be painted or renamed.
- Display: only on a Trophy Shelf (3 Planks + 1 Glass, holds 6). Plate reads "[Alias] · Worthy Rival · 5 matches." No custom text; Signs can't attach to a Trophy Shelf; bobbleheads can't be placed anywhere else.
- Friend control: Settings → "My bobbleheads" lists every one that exists of them with a Remove button; removal turns it into a Berty Bobblehead for the holder (nothing lost), with a neutral note "A bobblehead went back to Berty." Setting "Nobody can earn my bobblehead" (off by default; flipping it removes all existing ones the same way).
- Blocked players can never earn yours. Teachers can remove any bobblehead and see them all.

## 6. Music (tied to DJ Berty; no joke-noise instruments)
- **Tone Block:** 2 Planks + 1 Copper Wire. Tap to raise pitch (2 octaves, C major by default; Chromatic toggle). Instrument set by the block underneath: Wood = marimba, Stone = drum, Glass = chime, Steel = steel drum, Cloth = soft bass. Plays when tapped, stepped on, or triggered by a circuit (Circuit Daily tie-in: wire a song). Max 8 notes per second per area.
- **Instrument Blocks** (playable furniture; tap opens an 8-pad mini player): Keyboard (3 Planks + 1 Steel + 1 Copper Wire), Drum Kit (2 Cloth + 2 Steel + 1 Planks), Guitar (3 Planks + 1 Copper Wire). Audible 12 blocks.
- **Jukebox:** 4 Planks + 1 Glass + 1 Copper Wire + 1 Battery Cell. Plays Music Discs: Berty's starter mixes, plus every song the kid saved in DJ Berty (signed-in, same account) appears as a Disc automatically. Audible 16 blocks, volume slider, one Jukebox playing per 32-block area.
- Sound controls: every player has Music on/off and volume; class mode starts at low volume; teachers can mute all music for the class.
- Badges: First Tune (play 8 Tone Blocks in a row), Mixmaster (play your own DJ Berty song on a Jukebox).
