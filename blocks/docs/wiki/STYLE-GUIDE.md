# Wiki and in-game voice
These rules apply to every wiki page, Bertodex line, chip, card and brief that a kid might read.

## Words
- **Plain words.** Write at a grade 5–6 reading level. Use short sentences with one idea each. Write "breaks" rather than "is destroyed", and "makes" rather than "produces".
- **Our own names only.** Never use Minecraft, Tekkit, mod names (BuildCraft, IndustrialCraft and so on), brand names, or other games' item names. Mod names may appear **only** in internal design docs as inspiration notes. They never appear in the game, the wiki or the Bertodex.
- **Say what it does first**, then the number. For example: "Holds 18 stacks." "Mines 4× faster than a hand."
- **Use the verbs exactly**: Move, Mine, Place, Use, Open, Connect, Pair, Move-item, Craft, Paint. Don't make up new ones ("activate", "interact").
- **Real vs Verge:** a fact chip must be true. If something is a Bertopia idea (glowing moss, teleport pads, floating islands), say so: "Real teleporting isn't possible…".
- **Kind and calm:** use no danger words such as kill, die, explode, weapon, or attack. "Bounce back to safety" is fine. Refusals are polite and tell you what to do next, for example "Needs power ⚡" or "Find one first".
- **Never colour-only:** wherever a colour carries meaning, the words or a pattern say it too.
- **Numbers:** use digits (3 Planks, 0.5 s), use metric with real units, and give times in ET.

## Format
- Wiki tables come from REGISTRY.json. Don't hand-edit generated pages.
- Each page starts with a one-line "what this is".
- Never put personal info, kids' real names or photos in the wiki. Use aliases only.
- Languages: the en text is the source. es, uk, ru, rw and ti are filled by Build per brief, and ar and fa-AF follow INTERACTION-STYLE §7. Curriculum Bot checks all of them.
