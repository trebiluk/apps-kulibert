# Bertopia Effects System (GameMaster, Oct 4 2026 6:52 AM ET)
> **Roles and worlds (6:56 AM):** "Teacher" means anyone Diego gives the TechWorks Teacher flag (staff, club officers, kid helpers), not a fixed staff list. "Creative" = the Build world (Teacher flag only) and "Survival" = the Game world (everyone). See BERTOPIA-WORLDS.md.

One system, no new controls. Every effect = **Use** an item (tap it in the hotbar, or tap the worn item's button). Teleport, Heal, Spawn/Summon, Morph, Camo and Wonder Lab ("magic") are all Effects. The Gravity Hat (Fun Items spec) is the Float effect and follows these rules.
All effect items are crafted at a **Lab Bench** (4 Planks + 2 Glass + 1 Steel) or earned (badges, Lucky Block); never bought with Cogs.

## Shared rules (every effect)
- **Levels I / II / III.** Higher levels use stronger ingredients ("more or stronger reagent = bigger or longer effect," the same lesson as the glow sticks). Upgrading: Lab Bench + the item + 1 upgrade reagent (II: Silicon; III: 2 Silicon + 1 Battery Cell).
- **Every effect has:** a duration, a cooldown (ring on the hotbar slot), and a colored visual ring under the player. Reduced motion shows a static icon only.
- **HUD chips:** active effects show as chips (top-left, 44 px) with a timer ring. Tap a chip to end it early.
- **Limits:** max 3 active effects at once; the same effect never stacks (using it again refreshes the duration).
- **Off zones (auto-end on entry):** contests, Daily challenges (Molten Chasm, Circuit Fix, Parkour Rush, Bertapult daily), and arenas. Exceptions: a teacher can switch on an "Effects Party" arena, and Hide-and-Seek uses its own fixed loadout.
- **Anti-griefing:**
  - No effect can move, change or damage another player or their build without that player's accept.
  - No effect works on land or storage you don't own, unless the owner allows crew.
  - Teachers always see true nameplates, and can turn off all effects for the class.
  - A rate limit on every effect stops spam.
- **Damage Off is the default**, so Heal matters only when Damage is turned on.

## 1. Teleport (player) — "Warp"
- **Warp Pad** (player-size): 4 Steel + 2 Glass + 2 Battery Cell + 1 Silicon. Max 3 per player. Placing one counts as a destination.
- **Warp Key** (the Use item): 1 Steel + 1 Copper Wire + 1 Glass.
- **How it works:** stand still for 3 s (cyan swirl) and you arrive at the chosen pad. Moving cancels it.
- **Levels:**
  - I: to your own Warp Pads. 5 min cooldown.
  - II: also "Meet Me": invite a crew member; it works only if they tap Accept, and you arrive next to them. 3 min cooldown.
  - III: 1.5 s wind-up. 90 s cooldown.
- **Energy:** each warp costs pad charges with the Teleport Pad math (1 / 2 / 3 by distance). Players can only warp to pads they own or to an accepted invite.
- **Off in:** all off zones and Hide-and-Seek.
- **Lesson:** "Farther = more energy. (Real scientists can only teleport information, not people!)"

## 2. Heal
- **Recipes:**
  - I Bandage: 1 Cloth makes 2.
  - II Herb Tonic: 1 Glass Vial + 2 Plant (any).
  - III Med Kit: 2 Cloth + 1 Herb Tonic + 1 Steel.
- **Levels:**
  - I: +2 hearts instantly. 10 s cooldown.
  - II: +4 hearts over 5 s. 20 s cooldown.
  - III: +6 hearts and +2 for crew within 4 blocks. 30 s cooldown.
- **Visual:** green sparkles. Arena: Heal I is allowed only in Effects Party arenas.
- **Lesson:** "Your body heals itself; rest, food and first aid help it." Keep it to basic first aid, with no medicine claims.

## 3. Spawn and Summon
- **Spawn point:** a Sleeping Bag (3 Cloth) sets your respawn point when you tap it. You can have 1 personal spawn point. The world owner sets the World Spawn, and a Crew Banner (2 Cloth + 1 Planks) adds a shared crew spawn point.
- **Summon** (Whistle Bell: 1 Copper + 1 Steel) calls only YOUR own companions:
  - I: wakes the Pet Rock and the Bot; the Bot does its catch-up hop.
  - II: also recalls your Drone home.
  - III: the Bot arrives instantly. The Pet Rock still rolls; slowness is its joke.
  - 60 s cooldown.
- **Critter Jar** (1 Glass + 1 Plant): releases friendly ambient critters (fireflies, butterflies, songbirds, frogs).
  - I: 3 critters. II: 6. III: 10 critters that glow at night.
  - They last 10 min. Cap: 20 critters per 32-block area. Only on your own or crew land.
  - Critters don't attack, block paths or follow other players.
- **Never** summons a hostile creature, and never summons onto someone else's land.
- **Lesson:** "Habitats: critters show up where there's food, water and shelter." Kids can plant flowers to attract butterflies.

## 4. Morph
- **Morph Vial:** 1 Glass Vial + 1 Glow Mix + 1 sample of the form (a block you own, or a Critter Jar for creatures).
- **Levels:**
  - I: Block form. You turn into any block you've collected; you can slide-move and hold still. 30 s. 60 s cooldown.
  - II: Creature form, with that creature's move: Frog jumps 2 blocks; Bird glides (falls 1 block/s); Prairie Dog (Alice!) digs dirt 2x faster; Cat lands on any drop without bouncing. 60 s. 90 s cooldown.
  - III: Either form for 120 s, plus holding still for 3 s gives Camo II for free. 120 s cooldown.
- **While morphed:** you can Move and Use; you can't Mine, Place, Open storage or Paint (tapping ends the morph).
- **Never** morph into another player's avatar or a person. Only blocks and the friendly creature set.
- **Visibility:** outside Hide-and-Seek, your alias floats above you whenever you move or are within 4 blocks. Teachers always see it.
- **Visual:** purple puff (Berty's accent).
- **Lesson:** "Mimicry: some animals copy the look of other things to stay safe."

## 5. Camo
- **Camo Cloak** (worn, Use to toggle): 3 Cloth + 1 Glass + 1 Paint dab.
- **Levels:**
  - I: 50% see-through while standing still.
  - II: 80% still, 30% moving.
  - III: fully blends with the block behind you after 3 s still.
  - 60 s duration; cooldown equals the duration.
- **Always:** a faint shimmer outline (never truly invisible). Outside Hide-and-Seek, your nameplate never hides, so Camo is just for looks there. Teachers see everyone.
- **Lesson:** "Camouflage: matching color and pattern to the background bends what eyes notice."

## 6. Hide-and-Seek (game mode, uses Morph + Camo + Tag)
- **Setup:** a Game Zone Flag (4 Cloth + 1 Planks) marks a boundary of 16–48 blocks; the host picks the size.
- **Loadout:** everyone gets the same loadout during the round, so it's fair. Hiders get Morph III and Camo II. Seekers get "Warmer/Colder" pings every 30 s (an arrow tint and sound, no exact spot). Personal effect items don't work inside the zone.
- **Rounds:** 30 s to hide, then 3 min to seek. 1 seeker per 5 players (1–3 seekers).
- **Tag:** tap a hider within 2 blocks; this reuses the Arena "Tag" gesture. A tagged hider joins the seekers ("Found!"), never "out."
- **Score:** seconds hidden and hiders found. An optional "just for fun" board on a class tab.
- **Rules:** inside the zone, only aliases show and nameplates hide for hiders. There's no chat beyond preset quick-callouts. Kids can leave anytime, and the host or a teacher can end the round.
- **Hosting:** anyone can start a game in the Game world by placing a Game Zone Flag; teachers can join, watch or stop any game (Diego, 6:53 AM). Games never run in the Build world.

## 8. Badges
