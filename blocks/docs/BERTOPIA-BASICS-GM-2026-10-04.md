## Glow stick TIERS (2026-10-04 6:34 AM ET): these supersede the single "Glow Stick (base)" rules above
All tiers: Survival times are real time; in Creative every tier is permanent at full radius. Each tier ends as a never-dark radius-1 'spent' marker that stays on the minimap until picked up.
- T1 Glow Pebble (breadcrumb): 1 Glow Moss makes 8. Radius 2, bright 5 min, then spent. Picking it up leaves nothing (the moss biodegrades). Cap 128 per player per world. Lesson: 'A tiny bit of chemical = a short glow.'
- T2 Glow Stick: 1 Plastic Tube + 1 Glow Mix makes 4. Plastic Tube is early: 2 Corn → 1 Bioplastic (Oven), 1 Bioplastic → 2 Tubes (Workbench); no Smelter (GM-ANSWERS-2026-10-06 §3). Before ores ship, Glow Mix also = 2 Glow Moss. Glow Mix = 1 Glow Moss + 1 Copper dust, or found in caves. Radius 3, bright 10 min, dims to radius 2 over the next 10, then spent. A spent stick returns 1 Plastic Tube. Lesson: 'More chemical = longer glow.'
- T3 Jumbo Glow: 1 Plastic Tube + 2 Glow Mix + 1 Booster Dye makes 2. Booster Dye = 1 Glow Moss + 1 Paint dab (Workbench); Paint dab = 1 Berry → 2 (Workbench), see GM-ANSWERS-2026-10-06 §1. Radius 5, bright 25 min, dims over 15, then spent (about 40 min total). A spent stick returns 1 Plastic Tube (2 per craft). Lesson: 'A glow stick mixes two chemicals. The reaction gives its energy to a dye, and the dye glows. The dye picks the color.'
- T4 Cold Glow Vial: 1 Glass Vial + 3 Glow Mix + 1 Ice makes 1. Radius 4, bright 60 min, dims over 20, then spent. A spent vial returns the Glass Vial. Lesson: 'Cold slows a reaction down. It glows dimmer, but it lasts much longer.'
- Colors: T1–T3 teal (default), blue, green, yellow, pink, purple, picked at craft. T4 is icy white-cyan only.
- Caps: T2–T4 share 64 active per player per world; past the cap the oldest spent one is auto-removed with a toast.

## Berty's Bot H1 Cargo Bay
- Recipe: Bot = 4 Steel + 2 Copper Wire + 2 Battery Cell + 1 Glass. Fob = 1 Steel + 1 Copper Wire; tap the bot with the fob to pair. Lesson line: 'The fob sends a radio signal; the bot follows the strongest signal.'
- Slots: 9 to start; Cargo Bay II (+2 Steel + 1 Chest) gives 18; Cargo Bay III (+4 Steel + 1 Chest + 1 Battery Cell) gives 27. Items are never lost.
- Follow: trails 2–3 blocks behind and stops when you stop. If it's stuck 5 s or more than 24 blocks away, it catch-up hops to the nearest safe block behind you, only while the fob is on you.
- Terrain: walks stairs, slopes and 1-block steps. Waits at ladders, 2+ ledges and gaps, then hops when you land. Water: floats and paddles, never dives, and waits on the surface. Lava and fire count as walls; if it's somehow in lava, it pops back to its last safe block. Players can't push or break it.
- Fob dropped, bot lost, or logout: the bot parks, shows a minimap beacon and waits forever. A dropped fob never despawns. If the fob is destroyed, craft a new one and re-pair; the old fob goes dead. Tap-hold packs the bot into an item with its contents intact.
- Limits: 1 per player per world. Access: Private (default), Crew deposit-only, or Crew open; teachers can always view. Auto-parks outside arenas. Name tag shows the alias only.
- Battery: 20 min of active following per charge; idle doesn't drain it. Recharge 4 min in daylight or 2 min on a Charger, plus auto-charge while parked in daylight. At 20%, the Bot icon gets a steady amber ring (`--warn`) plus the words 'Low battery', announced politely once. At 0%, it keeps a steady ring and says 'Battery empty, half speed'. Never blink and never use color alone; the inventory always opens.
- Creative: in the menu, always charged. Survival: crafted only, never bought.
- Badge: Robot Buddy on the first pairing.
