# 1. Holiday / seasonal packs: one class, about seven home cultures
Room reality: families speak Spanish, Ukrainian, Russian, Arabic, Dari, Kinyarwanda, Tigrinya and English. Some families do not celebrate Halloween or Christmas for religious reasons. Calendars differ (for example, Orthodox Christmas on Jan 7 and Nowruz in March).

**Recommendation (one rule set):**
1. **Seasons, not holidays, are the default.** Packs are named for the season: "Autumn" (hay bales, gourds, pumpkins, corn stalks, leaf piles, scarecrow) and "Winter" (snow décor, string lights). Nothing in a default pack names a holiday.
2. **Lights are building parts, all year.** Call them "String lights" (in the Winter pack also shown as "Winter lights"), plus lanterns. Kids can light up a build for whatever they celebrate, any month. No "Christmas lights" label anywhere in the game.
3. **Spooky is its own optional pack.** "Spooky" (friendly props, carved-face pumpkins) is a teacher toggle, ON by default only from mid-Oct to Nov 1, and any kid can simply not use it. No gore, no weapons, no skulls borrowed from other cultures (for example, Day of the Dead sugar skulls are not "spooky" props).
4. **Pumpkin carving is a craft, not a holiday.** A carved pumpkin is an "Autumn" item; kids can carve any face, pattern or letter. Same LogoLab Mark Builder rules: no words that break class rules (teacher can hide a carving).
5. **No religious symbols in any pack** (crosses, stars of David, crescents, nativity, menorahs). Kids may still build their own designs with normal blocks.
6. **Teacher control:** Hub Master Control gets one row per pack (Autumn, Spooky, Winter) with On / Off / Auto by date. Seasonal items already placed never vanish when a pack turns off; they just can't be crafted.
7. **No holiday contests.** Build contests can use a season ("Autumn build-off") but never a single religious holiday.

Check, not blocking: Diego may want to glance at Solvay UFSD's board policy on holiday and religious observances (unverified, I didn't find the policy number; ask the principal or check the district BoardDocs page).
