Score · Grades 5–8 unit

BertyScore · BS 0.2.9

The song is on the staff. Kids tap the staff to write a note. Higher on the lines is a higher note. It saves on the Chromebook. Export downloads a file. Beats and Lights use that same song. The word Now names the note.

Guided start

  1. Press Play. Sound can stay off. Point at Now.
  2. Change one note. The staff redraws. Press Play.
  3. Grade 7 and 8: press Beats. Drums stay. F, B, and high C move to the nearest grid note, and the page says so.

Accommodations

Same class plan as the unit page. Deaf and hard of hearing students read Now. Low vision can zoom. Reduced motion still moves the word. Multilingual learners see the letter, not a solfege name. Extra tools are not required for the first prove.

Do not put a plan, a label, or a legal name on this screen. Alias is a nickname.
