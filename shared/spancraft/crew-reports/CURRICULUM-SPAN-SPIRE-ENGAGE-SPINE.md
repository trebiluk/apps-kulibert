# SpanCraft + Spire Lab — engage spine

**Course home:** Solvay Middle School Technology · grades 6–8  
**Doors:** [SpanCraft](https://apps.kulibert.net/spancraft/) **SC 1.3.4** · [Spire Lab](https://apps.kulibert.net/spire-lab/) **SL 1.3.3**  
**Owner:** teach doc only · **HOLD app build** unless a later GO names a door change  
**This file was not in the repo.** It records the spine the live doors already teach. It does not add a lesson.

## Kid line

Open SpanCraft. Drag a deck across the gap. Press Test. Read the word. Then open Spire Lab. Drop the slab. Read the height.

## Spine

| Step | Door | Do this | Read this |
|------|------|---------|-----------|
| 1 | SpanCraft | Drag a Deck from bank to bank. | The caption, before any count. |
| 2 | SpanCraft | Press Test. | **Pass** or **Fail**, plus a mark. Sound is not the grade. |
| 3 | SpanCraft | If it misses, change one thing. Press Test again. | The same word. Stars come after a pass: one if it holds, two on budget, three if the path is clean. |
| 4 | SpanCraft | Pass the first gap. | The toy is named: “Sticky joint is yours.” The chip shows that name. |
| 5 | Spire Lab | Drop the slab on the stack. | **Stand** or **Miss**, plus the height. |
| 6 | Spire Lab | Keep dropping. | Goal is the dashed line at 10. An even drop adds a streak. A miss clears it. |
| 7 | Spire Lab | Reach the first stack, then the goal line. | The toy is named in the caption. |

Gap Isles and Stand Peak are the same spine on a map. A locked card says the next toy. A card you have says **Yours**. Bets stay optional. They never block the first toy.
