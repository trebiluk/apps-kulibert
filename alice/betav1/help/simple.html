How to play

Two people play on one screen. You need each other.

Alice: A and D to walk. W to jump. Q to use. E to call.

Hare: arrow keys to walk. Up to jump. Comma to use. Period to call.

The hare jumps high. It can pull the lever. Alice is strong. She can push the box and move the big board.

Pull the lever. The gate goes up. Push the box under the gate. Move the board. Both go to the door.
