How to play

Two players share one screen. Neither of you can finish alone.

Alice (prairie dog): A / D move, W jump, Q use, E call.

Wonderland (hare): ← / → move, ↑ jump, comma use, period call.

Wonderland can jump high and reach the lever on the ledge. Alice is strong: she pushes the crate and hauls the plank.

Pull the lever to lift the gate. Push the crate under the gate so it can't close. Then haul the plank and both reach the door.
