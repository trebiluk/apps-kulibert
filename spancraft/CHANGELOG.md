## 1.1.0 — Stars — 2026-09-22

For class:

- Stage 1 only. Drag a part. It stretches, then drops. Grips are 44px.
- One prove: Test hangs the load. Pass or fail is a word plus a mark, in under two seconds.
- 1 star if it passes. 2 stars if the build is at or under the part budget. 3 stars if the load path is clean.
- After the first star, a student can tap the joint they think fails, then Test.
- A 3-star build stays as a faint outline on this Chromebook.
- Assist stays on the screen. Retry keeps the build.
- No sound is required.

Hard refresh if a cart still says Load prove.
