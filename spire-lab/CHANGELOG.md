## 1.1.0 — Streak — 2026-09-22

For class:

- One prove: drop the slab. Height is how many stay standing.
- Drag the grip. The line stretches from the hook. A long pull draws a spike instead of a band.
- An even drop (within about a tenth of the width) adds a calm streak. A miss or a crooked drop clears it.
- No sound is required. Assist stays on the screen. Retry starts the tower again.
- Stock tower pictures and sounds are not in this app.
