How to use it

  • ▣ Tap a tool on the side bar or bottom bar.
  • ▶ Press the big Play button. It stays in the same corner.
  • ✓ A picked picture has a check.
  • ◉ Each picture button has a name. A check shows the one you picked.
  • 👁 Every word is easy to read. All buttons fit above the play bar. 🎤 Listening. Then the mic turns off.
  • 🥁 Make a beat and New beat open your beat. Rock loads only on a fresh start or when you tap Rock. Clear stays clear until you pick a style or tap a pad.
  • 🎤 The mic line shows if the mic is on, listening, or blocked.
  • 🎨 Looks opens the picture names, colors, wall, and frame. Surprise me mixes them.
  • Swipe up on the row under the picture to find the Sound buttons.
  • Swipe the row of pictures sideways to see them all.
  • 🧩 Pick the Code look. Then you can change its folds and size.
  • ▶ Press Play. The picture waits, then your music becomes light.
  • 🖼 The frame glows a little with the kick. It stays still if motion is reduced.
  • ⚡ When the beat drops, the picture jumps to your next look for one bar, then comes back. Drop swap can turn it off. It stays still if motion is reduced.
  • 🎚 Each loudness slider says the drum name.
  • 📝 The Score tab teaches you in your language.
  • Swipe the beat boxes sideways to reach the last beats.
  • 🥁 The Tap tab teaches you in your language.
  • 🥁 Rests, warm-ups and the Band tab teach you in your language.
  • 🎚 Sound sliders, files and pictures explain themselves in your language.
  • 🎉 Surprise beats and remix ideas talk to you in your language.
  • 📋 Practice and class lists talk to you in your language.
  • 🥁 The finger pads show their names in your language.
  • 📽 Perform hides the side list, counts 3-2-1 in big numbers, then only the light show. The mic stays off unless you tap it. Less motion shows Ready.
  • 🎬 Perform counts 3, 2, 1. Players don't get extra notes.
  • 🎤 Mic lights starts off. Tap it to turn the room mic on or off. If you say no, the song lights still move. Nothing is recorded or sent.
