# Music changelog

**Chip: MU 2.15.0** · 2026-09-27 · channel **live**

Play says Play. Stop says Stop. The note letters are big keys. The four choices under them are 1 beat, Fast, Longer, and Rest.

**Chip: MU 2.14.0** · 2026-09-27 · channel **live**
