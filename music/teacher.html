Teacher · not a kid screen

Music · MU 1.0.0

One screen for grades 5–8. On a wide Chromebook the notes and the drums are on the left. The picture is on the right. On a phone the picture is on top. Students switch Notes and Drums with the two icons.

Press Play with sound off. The word still says Beat 1 and Kick or the note letter. That is enough to pass. Do not ask if they heard it.

The menu holds tempo, the title, and the picture style. The title is four menus. Students do not type it.

A kick on beat 1 is the pulse. Higher on the staff is a higher note. Tempo is speed. It does not change the count.
