# Volt Duel changelog

VD 1.0.5 — Run and jump buttons hold on phones and fit on every screen. Folds in c90db75 and 96864bb.
VD 1.0.6 — VEX is a circle with V and RIO is a square with R. A sign shows who went back to a checkpoint. The countdown, checkpoints, and the flag are read aloud. Rematch starts that heat over.
VD 1.0.7 — Touch pads stay on an upright phone. Menu, Sign in, and Home stay easy to tap. A held pad shows a new icon and the word On.
VD 1.0.8 — VEX and RIO score chips differ by shape and letter. RIO no longer covers VEX at the start. Staff Test mode can reach a win, a pit, and Rematch.
