What's new

Version 1.7.0

  • Level 11, Touch: a cube rides a belt. The claw has a touch sensor, and there is a new If touch block.
  • Close the claw without touch and the cube slips. A cube set down off-center tips over, and a stack can fall. The note names the step.
  • The cube now moves by physics in fixed 1/120 s steps, so a run takes the same time on every screen.

Version 1.6.1

  • Every old /pinchy/ address now opens Berty & Bizzy.
  • The tips sit under what they point at, so they never cover other text.

Version 1.6.0

  • Sync A, B and C make the faster arm wait for the other arm. There are also Wait for other arm and Wait 0.5 s.
  • A result card shows cycle time, blocks used and parts moved, and your best on this device.
  • Three new levels: Sync it, Other way and Two cubes.

Version 1.5.0

  • Bizzy, a second robot arm, joins Berty. Each arm has 5 joints.
  • Level 7, Hand-off: Berty picks up the cube and Bizzy places it. Each arm has its own lane of blocks, and both lanes run together.
  • If the arms touch, the note names the two parts. Levels 1 to 6 play with Berty only.

Version 1.4.0

  • Pinchy is now Berty & Bizzy. The old address /pinchy/ sends you here.
  • Berty and Bizzy wave hello once per visit. Tips and a Help button explain the blocks.

Version 1.3.2

  • All 8 languages now translate the status line, E-STOP, Clear, the joint summary, the claw readout, the stars, blocks and time, the top bar, the theme button, and What's new.
  • The language you pick is kept and shows from the first screen. Words that are not English are a machine draft.

Version 1.3.1

  • The arm is printed links, six servos, and a two-jaw gripper.
  • One Menu button in every app. It opens that app's menu.

Version 1.3.0

  • Move a robot arm.
  • Paper pendant: warm paper, charcoal ink, and mustard only on the header bars, the arm, and the E-STOP band.
  • One Menu button in every app. It opens that app's menu.

Version 1.2.2

  • Move a robot arm.
  • The empty gap above the status line is gone.
  • On a short sideways screen the level names stay whole, and the program stays on screen.
  • Spanish, Arabic, and Dari name the blocks and the help. Those words are a machine draft.

Version 1.2.1

  • Move a robot arm.
  • The 3D scene fills the width, and the labels on it are large enough to read.
  • Base left and Base right have Spanish, Arabic, and Dari names. Those names are a machine draft.
  • Retry clears the stars on the row and in the words.

Version 1.2.0

  • Move a robot arm.
  • The arm is drawn in 3D from a fixed camera, so the base can turn.
  • Levels 1 to 6 are unchanged. Stars stay on this screen only.

Version 1.1.0

  • Move a robot arm.
  • Level 4 sorts a pile with Repeat. Level 5 sends a red square to bin A and a blue circle to bin B. Level 6 fixes a broken program with swaps.
  • Wrist up and Wrist down are in the later levels. A crash names the wrist.
  • Stars mean done, under the block par, and under the time par. They stay on this screen only.

Version 1.0.0

  • Pinchy McPinchface is the arm game.
  • Level 1 moves one joint. Level 2 picks up a part and places it. Level 3 goes to a spot from A1 to C3.
  • If the arm hits something, the note names the part and the step stays outlined. Retry keeps your program.

Arrow keys pick a block. Enter adds it. Q turns the base left in 3D. E turns the base right in 3D. R runs. Z undoes. Retry keeps the program.

One Menu button in every app. It opens that app's menu.
